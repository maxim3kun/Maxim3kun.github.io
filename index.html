🌐 General

  • @bot <msg>Talk directly to the bot
  • !image <desc>Generate an image
  • !say <msg>Make the bot speak
  • !helloGreeting
  • !poll <q> | 1 | 2Create a poll (up to 9 options)

🎉 Fun

  • !complimentRandom compliment 💖
  • !jokeRandom joke 😄
  • !encouragementMotivation 💪
  • !hugVirtual hug 🤗
  • !8ball <q>Magic 8-ball 🎱
  • !dice [faces]Roll the dice 🎲
  • !conspiracy [topic]Conspiracy theory 🕵️
  • !joke frAppend fr or es to change language

🎂 Birthdays

  • !birthday add DD/MMSave your birthday
  • !birthday listView all birthdays
  • !birthday remove [@user]Remove a birthday
  • !help birthdayFull details

🎮 Mini-games

  • !minesweeper [easy|medium|hard]Minesweeper 💣
  • !geo [easy|medium|hard]Geography quiz 🌍
  • !geo stopStop the quiz
  • !triviaGeneral knowledge quiz 🧠
  • !guessnumberNumber guessing 🎯
  • !connect4 solo / @userConnect 4 (react 1–7) 🔴🟡

🎵 Music — Suno AI

  • !music generator <prompt>Style, mood, lyrics 🎶

🎙️ Voice & Radio

  • !join / !leaveJoin / leave voice channel

  • !youtube <url>Play a YouTube video 🎬

  • !radio leaveDisconnect radio

  • !playlist play <name>Play a playlist
  • !karaoke <artist song>Synced karaoke 🎤
